# Stracker Sales Video — Production Plan

## Goal
75-second intro teaser for prospective B2B clients. First sales video. Hybrid style (initially) → pivoted to **full Manim animated UI** for v1 (no creds available for live recording).

## Narrative Arc
**Pain → Solution → Proof → CTA**

Sales teams drown in spreadsheets, texts, and scattered notes. Stracker puts the whole pipeline in one place — leads, visits, orders, billing — and gives your customers a clean portal too.

## Scene Breakdown

| # | Scene | Duration | Visual | Caption (on-screen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0-8s | Chaotic spreadsheet, sticky notes, scattered icons | "Sales shouldn't feel like this." |
| 2 | Logo Reveal | 8-14s | Stracker logo scales in, brand colors settle | "Meet **Stracker**." |
| 3 | Dashboard | 14-28s | Animated dashboard: KPI cards, lead map, revenue chart | "Your pipeline, in one view." |
| 4 | Lead → Deal | 28-42s | Lead card transforms into deal card with status pipeline | "From lead to closed, tracked automatically." |
| 5 | Billing Portal | 42-58s | Animated client portal: invoices, services, payment | "Your customers get a clean portal too." |
| 6 | CTA | 58-75s | Logo + URL + tagline | "strackerapp.com — Sales, organized." |
